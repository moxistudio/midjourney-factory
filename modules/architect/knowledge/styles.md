# Midjourney Style Reference

Use these style anchors to diversify generated prompts. Combine them with lighting, composition, and mood keywords.

## Cinematic Lighting
- dramatic key light and rim light
- film grain, lens flare, deep shadows
- widescreen composition, high contrast

## Synthwave
- neon pink and cyan palette
- retro-futuristic skyline, grid horizon
- 1980s aesthetic, chrome reflections

## Studio Ghibli
- painterly textures, hand-drawn warmth
- whimsical scenery, soft natural light
- cozy atmosphere, expressive character design
